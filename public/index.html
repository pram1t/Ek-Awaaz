How Ek Awaaz helps

Questions people ask

Clear answers, before you start.

Ek Awaaz is built to make the process visible—not add another layer of uncertainty.

Do I have to pick a ministry or a category?+

No. Tell us what happened and where. The route is suggested in plain language for you to review.

Is filing a grievance always my best option?+

Not always. When a sector has a stronger statutory remedy or ombudsman, we show that path before you file.

Do you need my Aadhaar?+

No. The prototype uses mock identity details only and never asks for Aadhaar, PAN, payment information, or a real OTP.

What is real here, and what is mocked?+

Routing logic, remedy guidance, and case joining are demonstrated in this prototype. Filing into government systems and identity verification are mocked.
